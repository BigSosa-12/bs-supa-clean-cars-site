FAQ

Quick answers.

Where do you work?

Battle Creek, Michigan and the surrounding 30-mile area. If you're unsure, request a free quote with your town or ZIP code.

What do the packages include?

Standard Service: vacuum, wipe down, protectors and shampoo. Deep Clean: the same as Standard plus a drill brush for oil grime, pet hair removal, etc. Supa Clean: all of the above plus new car smells, leather shines, pet hair removal, and a tire pressure check & service note. See the prices for cars and SUVs.

How much is a deposit?

50% of the service you select is due when booking, and it is refunded if you cancel. Each package above has its own Pay deposit button that opens Stripe's secure checkout.

Does paying the deposit book my appointment?

No. Payment alone does not reserve a time. Send an appointment request and we'll confirm a day and time with you. Your appointment is booked once we confirm it.

Is the quote really free?

Yes. Free quotes are US$0 for cars and SUVs.

Do SUVs cost more?

SUVs have their own prices: Standard US$175, Deep Clean US$225, Supa Clean US$250.

Do you store my card details?

No. Deposits are paid on Stripe's hosted checkout page. This website never collects or sees card numbers.
