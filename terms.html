Booking terms

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Services and prices

B's Supa Clean Cars LLC provides mobile interior detailing in Battle Creek, Michigan and the surrounding 30-mile area. Prices for cars: Standard Service US$150, Deep Clean Package US$185, Supa Clean Package US$225. Prices for SUVs: SUV Standard Service US$175, SUV Deep Clean Package US$225, SUV Supa Clean Package US$250. What each package includes is listed on the services and pricing section of our home page.

Free quote

Quotes are free (US$0) for cars and SUVs. Request one through the form on our home page.

Deposit

50% of the service you select is due when booking. The deposit is refunded if you cancel. Deposits are paid through Stripe's hosted checkout; the deposit amount for each package is shown beside its "Pay deposit" button.

How your appointment is set

Your appointment is set by your deposit and the day we agree on. Send an appointment request with the day you'd like so we can agree on a day with you. Any other scheduling details are covered when we agree on your day.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe on stripe.com. This website does not collect card details.

Photos

Photos on this site show past jobs. Every vehicle is different, so results on your vehicle may vary.

Changes

We may update these terms and prices. The version on this page when you book applies to your booking.

We don't use cookies.
