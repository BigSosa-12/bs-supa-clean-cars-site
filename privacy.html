Privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

B's Supa Clean Cars LLC ("we", "us") is a mobile interior detailing business based in Battle Creek, Michigan. This policy explains what this website does with your information.

What we collect

When you send an appointment or quote request

The request form asks for your name, an email address and/or phone number, the service you want, the kind of vehicle, whether the interior is leather or upholstery, its condition on a 1 to 10 scale, your town or ZIP code, a convenient day, and optionally the year, make and model, a preferred time of day, and a message. This information is sent to us only when you press "Send appointment request".

When you pay a deposit

The "Pay deposit" buttons open Stripe's hosted checkout page on stripe.com. You enter payment details on Stripe, not on this website. We never receive or store your full card number. Stripe shares payment records with us, such as the amount paid and the contact details you enter at checkout. Stripe's own privacy policy applies to what it collects.

Technical data

Our website host may keep standard server logs (for example IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and reliability.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request, give a quote, and confirm a day and time with you.
  • To keep business records of services and deposits.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who receives it

Your request is sent from your browser to FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-handling service that emails it to us at the business email address. Stripe processes deposits. Our website host serves the pages. These providers handle data only to provide those services.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising pixels or other trackers. See . If we add optional analytics or marketing tools in future, they will be blocked until you accept them, and you will be able to change your choice at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the details you sent us. Contact us through the request form on our home page and mention that your message is a privacy request.

Children

This website is intended for adults booking services for their own vehicles.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
